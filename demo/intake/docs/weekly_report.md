# Bloom & Basket weekly operations report · 2026 week 43 (19 to 25 October)

A fictional shop's fictional report, used to show how the skill takes causes from a document. The shop writes
in Chinese; this is a translation for readers of the repository. Proposals quote the Chinese file, because a
quote must appear word for word in the document it cites.

## This week

Revenue is still down on last year, and the pressure is still on the marketplace side. The app and our website
are holding up; weekend orders were higher than weekdays, as every year.

## Channels

From 12 October the marketplace applied new fee terms. We did not accept the new commission tier, the shop then
dropped out of the home-page placement, and search traffic fell clearly.
Talks with the marketplace continue; they will only review it next month.
The website had a checkout redesign this week; no change in conversion yet, we keep watching.

## Regions and delivery

Three days of heavy rain in the east from the 21st to the 23rd almost stopped same-day delivery in Shanghai and
Hangzhou; many customers switched to scheduled delivery.
Same-day delivery needs riders on the road, and heavy rain stops them: an old problem in the rainy season.
Other regions delivered as normal.

## Products

Fruit baskets sold particularly well this month: about half of new customers' orders were fruit baskets, which
pulled the average order value down a little.
Samples of the new gift-box packaging are back; launch expected in December.

## Other

From next Tuesday the support team changes its rota and adds one person in the evening.
