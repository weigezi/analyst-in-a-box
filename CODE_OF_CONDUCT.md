# Code of conduct

[中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh.md)

Be kind and be specific. Disagree with the work, not the person. Do not harass anyone, and do not post other
people's private information or business data. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or pull requests that break
these rules and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, open an issue marked "conduct" or
contact the maintainer through the address on the GitHub profile.
